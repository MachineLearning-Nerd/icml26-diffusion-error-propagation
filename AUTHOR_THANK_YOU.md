# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Nail B. Khelifa, Richard E. Turner, and Ramji Venkataramanan for
making *Quantifying Error Propagation and Model Collapse in Diffusion Models*
and its theoretical and empirical claims available for independent study. The
paper's separation of score-error propagation, fresh-data fraction, and
distributional drift made it possible to build a claim-specific audit with
formal certificates, controls, and honest protocol boundaries.

This repository is an independent reproduction audit. It is not an official
implementation and does not imply author review or endorsement.
